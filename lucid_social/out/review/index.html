Before posting

Notes

Set before posting

  • Launch date: the plan counts back from launch day (L-21 to L-1).
  • Instagram handle (preview shows @thelucidinstitute as a placeholder).
  • luciddaily.com waitlist page live, with the sign-up the posts promise.
  • Confirm 'small first run' and 'waitlist hears first' are true before F08 and F09 go out.

Claims

  • Only the label tagline is a structure/function claim. It always carries the asterisk and the full FDA statement (on the image and in the caption).
  • Ingredient slides state names, amounts and origin only, all from the Supplement Facts panel.
  • No disease, result, timeline or sexual-function language; no #lowT / #TRT style hashtags.
  • If any post is boosted as an ad, keep copy off personal attributes (no "men over 40", no "struggling with...").

Production

  • Type is set in Ashcroft (your licensed files from the packaging handoff) with the exact brand-book palette.
  • Small labels use Share Tech Mono as a stand-in for Mono45 Headline (Adobe Fonts); swap it in Figma for final art.
  • Product renders come from the final rev6 label artwork, not the older 60-capsule mockups in the brand book.
  • Masters are 1080 x 1350 (feed) and 1080 x 1920 (stories, reel) PNG in lucid_social/out/posts.

*These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.
